Scenario Explorer & Stress Tests

In the complete system, Kukule Ganga was tested on diverse hydrological regimes:

  • El Niño Drought: Severe dry season where active pondage is maintained above reserve. The Hybrid LP-DQN outperforms pure LP by preserving head and avoiding premature drawdown.
  • Flood Inflow Year: Continuous full capacity operation; pre-drawdown prevents flood wave cresting over the weir un-turbined.
  • Festival Seasons (Vesak & Poson): Demand weights surge to 1.15x; the optimizer shifts dispatch into peak demand hours.
  • Single Turbine Outages: Discharge capacity halved from 48 to 24 m³/s; optimizer smooths release to avoid avoidable spills.
