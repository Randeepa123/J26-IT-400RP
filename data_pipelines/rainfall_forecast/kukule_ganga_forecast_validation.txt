3. RAINFALL GENERATION METHOD
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Monte Carlo simulations: 1,000
  Random seed: 42

  Per-day procedure:
  1. Determine weekly forecast category (weekly_forecast.pdf primary source)
  2. Refine with seasonal outlook and El Nino analogue (secondary sources)
  3. Assign lognormal multiplier distribution: centre = category_mean,
     sigma = category_spread + 0.013 * lead_day
  4. Adjust wet-day probability: p_adj = p_clim x multiplier (clipped 0.05-0.98)
  5. Sample wet/dry occurrence; sample Gamma amounts for wet days
  6. Scale by catchment spatial factor (lognormal, mean=1.25)

  Category parameters:
  Category                       Centre   Base spread
  ----------------------------------------------------
  near_normal_lean_below           0.90          0.32
  near_normal                      1.00          0.30
  below_normal                     0.65          0.35

4. HYDROLOGICAL MODEL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Model    : SCS Curve Number + Linear Baseflow Recession
  Status   : UNCALIBRATED - no observed Kukule Ganga flow data was available
  Outputs  : PROVISIONAL SYNTHETIC ESTIMATES ONLY

  Catchment area    : 270 km2
  CN (mean/std)     : 80 / 5 (normal distribution)
  Antecedent cond.  : AMC-III (wet, based on Aug 2026 near/above normal in Sab.)
  Baseflow          : normal(27, 8) m3/s
  Recession coeff K : uniform(0.87, 0.93) per day
  Direct frac       : uniform(0.55, 0.75) of surface runoff
  Subsurface release: uniform(0.40, 0.60) per day from storage bucket

  High-inflow threshold: 100 m3/s (PROVISIONAL)
  Basis: approx design Q for 70 MW @ ~90 m head:
         P = rho*g*Q*H*eta => Q = 70e6/(1000*9.81*90*0.85) = 93 m3/s
  NOTE: This is NOT an official operational flood threshold.

  Mass balance: inflow_m3 = inflow_m3s * 86400 seconds/day
  Output CSV stores volumes in Mm3 (= m3 / 1e6)

6. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  a) ECMWF MAPS NOT NUMERICALLY READABLE
     The 14 ECMWF forecast maps are colour-coded graphical images.
     Exact catchment-average rainfall requires GIS spatial averaging.
     This was not possible. The ECMWF guidance is QUALITATIVE only.

  b) SYNTHETIC RAINFALL CALIBRATION
     Gamma parameters were fitted to SYNTHETIC daily data derived from
     ~10-day CHIRPS aggregates. Daily-scale statistics may be unreliable.

  c) SPATIAL MISMATCH
     Ratnapura district (~3100 km2) vs Kukule Ganga catchment (~270 km2).
     The scaling factor (1.25) is an estimate, not a calibrated value.

  d) HYDROLOGICAL MODEL UNCALIBRATED
     CN, baseflow, and routing parameters are literature estimates.
     Model uncertainty is VERY HIGH. Inflow scenarios could be wrong
     by a factor of 2 or more without calibration data.

  e) ANTECEDENT CONDITIONS
     Initial catchment wetness assumed from Aug 2026 above-normal context.
     No Sep 2026 rainfall data was available to refine this assumption.

  f) 1 NOVEMBER 2026 (BEYOND ECMWF DAILY MAPS)
     No daily ECMWF map exists for this date. Forecast uses weekly W3
     (below-normal) with widened uncertainty (+0.013*lead_day spread).
