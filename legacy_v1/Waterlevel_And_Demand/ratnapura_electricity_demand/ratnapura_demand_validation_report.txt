2. SOURCE INVESTIGATION & DATA AVAILABILITY AUDIT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Public Utility Data Access Audit:
  * Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB): Public website publishes aggregate annual provincial
    and divisional sales in the CEB Statistical Digest and Data Books. Sub-annual daily
    metering telemetry at the Area/District level is retained in internal SCADA/MDMS systems
    and is NOT accessible via public APIs or web portals.
  * EDL Care Portal: Scrapes from edlcare.edl.lk reflect national generation/dispatch, not
    Ratnapura retail consumption.
  * Research Action: To support Linear Programming (LP) and Deep Q-Network (DQN) optimization,
    a defensible 5-year daily scenario was synthesized using official CEB annual sales benchmarks.
